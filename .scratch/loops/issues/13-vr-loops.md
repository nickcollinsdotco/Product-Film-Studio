## Decisions (grilling, 2026-10-14)

Ticket 09's VR Bookend was proof only: a double zoom that never settled. These Loops fix that.

1. **Motion rule:** one subject, at most one push-in; the Camera settles on the Notice for at least 1s, then returns; a Scroll runs at constant zoom. Part of the ship bar (ADR-0012 amended, Loop card guidance updated).
2. **Engine:** the Library. First port Quick Loop's render fixes into it: zoom by resizing rather than scaling, AA edge margin, layered shadow that fades with zoom, seeded dither, PNG-sequence + error-diffusion encode (Final renders only; Drafts keep the fast path). Quick Loop itself stays as it is.
3. **Slate:** start with three, all browser frame, pink Backdrop, one Screen each, Bookend:
   - Hero push-in: `home-hero`, Hold pushing in on the player.
   - Home scroll: `home-full`, Scroll at constant zoom from the hero to find-next or look-closer (Claude picks), one push-in, back. Replaces the 09 Loop.
   - Step inside: `home-stepinside-matterhorn`, slow push-in on the Matterhorn panel.
   Add Immersion (Hold) and Creators (Tour) only if the first three pass review. Hero variants and Matterhorn → VR need two Screens: out of v1.
4. **Frame size:** Landscape 16:9 only.
5. **Tilt:** per Loop. Each is drafted at 0° and 8° side by side; Nick picks.
6. **Review:** a static HTML review page, generated by one script: every Draft autoplaying at card size beside the Steel Hat phone clip.
7. **Length:** about 8s each, the 1s settle included.
8. **Focus areas:** Claude proposes areas for the single-viewport Screens and the scroll target; Nick judges them in the Drafts, with no separate overlay approval.

Flow: port → areas + Loop cards → Drafts (0° and 8°) → review page → Nick picks → Final renders (≤ 6 MB) → Nick uploads to the portfolio.
